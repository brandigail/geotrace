# Assets to replace or review

No external images are stored in the repository, and none are hotlinked. The only things fetched from elsewhere are the map tiles, which are the map itself (OpenStreetMap, with the required credit shown on the map).

- **Demo trips:** three synthetic trips generated by `src/lib/seed.ts` along approximate real coordinates. They are not recorded GPS tracks. Replace them with real, consented tracks (for example from GPX files you recorded) if you want a more authentic demo.
- **Demo waypoint photos:** pictures drawn in the browser by `src/lib/photo.ts` and captioned "Sample photo (generated placeholder)". Replace with real photos if you have them.
- `public/icon.svg`, `public/favicon.svg` and the generated `pwa-*.png` / `apple-touch-icon.png`: a simple placeholder route icon. Replace with a designed logo if you have one.
